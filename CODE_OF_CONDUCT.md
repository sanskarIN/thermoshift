# Code of Conduct

ThermoShift uses a simple contributor covenant: participate respectfully, focus criticism on ideas and code, avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct, and protect private information shared during support or security work.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive, threatening, spammy, or privacy-violating content and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Include only the information needed to investigate the concern.
